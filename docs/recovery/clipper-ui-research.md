# KlipFlow clipper UI research

Sources reviewed on 2026-09-17:

- Whop Clips overview: https://whop.com/blog/whop-clips/
- TikTok Creator Rewards overview: https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/creator-rewards-program
- TikTok rewards help: https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-rewards-program/how-rewards-work

Relevant findings: Whop Clips positions the product around earning money per 1,000 views by creating or repurposing promotional videos. TikTok Creator Rewards emphasizes an easy-to-understand dashboard for eligibility, estimated rewards, and performance. The rebuild therefore prioritizes campaign discovery, rate per 1,000 views, example source video, submitted clips, and earnings instead of infrastructure terminology.
